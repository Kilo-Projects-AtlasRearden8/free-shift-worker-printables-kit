# Sleep Tracker

Log bedtime, wake time, hours slept, and sleep quality. Built for rotating shifts, night shifts, and irregular schedules.

## How to use

Fill in one row per day. Record when you went to bed, when you woke up, total hours slept, rate your sleep quality from 1–5, and note any naps. Use the Notes field for shift start times, caffeine, alarms, or anything that affected your sleep.

**Why it matters:** Irregular shifts disrupt sleep. A simple log helps you see what works — for example, which bedtime works best after a night shift — so you can protect your rest.

## Columns

| Column | What to record |
|--------|----------------|
| Date | Date |
| Bedtime | When you went to bed |
| Wake time | When you woke up |
| Hours slept | Total sleep time |
| Quality (1–5) | Rate your sleep, 1 = poor, 5 = great |
| Naps (min) | Nap duration in minutes |
| Notes | Shift start times, caffeine, alarms, etc. |

## Low-energy sleep tips for shift work

- Keep the room dark and cool.
- Try a consistent wind-down routine even when shift times change.
- Limit caffeine in the last half of your shift.
- Protect your sleep block with earplugs or a white-noise fan.
