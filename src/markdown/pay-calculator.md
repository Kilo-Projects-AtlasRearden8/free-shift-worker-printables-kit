# Pay Calculator

Work out gross pay, overtime, shift differentials, tax, and weekly/monthly estimates for a single shift.

## How to use

Enter your hourly rate, hours worked, shift differential percent, overtime threshold, overtime multiplier, and estimated tax percent. Results calculate automatically as you type in the HTML version. Print this page or save as PDF to keep a copy with your pay records.

**Example:** $30/hour • 12-hour night shift • 10% differential • overtime after 8 hours at 1.5× • 20% estimated tax.

## Fields

| Field | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| Hourly rate ($) | Your base pay per hour |
| Hours worked | Hours on this shift |
| Shift differential (%) | Extra percent paid for nights/weekends/etc. |
| Overtime threshold (hrs) | Hours after which overtime applies |
| Overtime multiplier | Overtime rate multiplier (e.g. 1.5) |
| Estimated tax (%) | Rough tax withholding to set aside |
| Shifts per week | Typical shifts worked per week |

## Results

- Regular hours / Overtime hours
- Base pay / Overtime pay / Shift differential
- Gross pay (this shift)
- Estimated tax
- Net pay (this shift)
- Gross per week / Net per week
- Gross per month (estimate) / Net per month (estimate)

**Estimate only.** Actual pay depends on your employer's policies, local tax withholding, benefits deductions, and labor laws. This tool is for personal planning — it is not payroll or tax advice.

**Privacy:** The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
