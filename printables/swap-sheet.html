Shift Swap Sheet

Request a shift swap, get coworker and manager sign-off, and keep a record of every trade.

← Back to kit

How to use: Complete one form per swap request. Fill in your original shift and the shift you want instead, name the coworker you're trading with, then get both signatures plus manager approval. File the completed sheet with your shift records.

Note: Check your workplace's swap policy before trading — some employers require manager pre-approval or restrict swaps between certain roles.
