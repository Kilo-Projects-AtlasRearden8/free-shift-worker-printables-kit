Shift Log

Track your shifts by month. Print blank, fill in by hand, or complete on screen before printing to PDF.

← Back to kit

How to use: Write one row per shift. Enter the date, day of week, shift type (days, nights, evenings, weekends), start and end times, unpaid break minutes, and total hours worked. At the end of the month, add up the Total Hours column.

Tip: Use Shift Type to spot patterns across days/nights — useful when you're building a sleep schedule or checking your pay stub.
