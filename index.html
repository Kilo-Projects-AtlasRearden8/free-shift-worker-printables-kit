Shift Worker Printables Kit

Free, print-ready forms for shift workers: shift logs, sleep trackers, shift swap sheets, and a pay calculator. Low-energy design, soft on the eyes, easy to print to PDF or paper. No sign-up, no tracking, nothing leaves your computer.

📅

Shift Log

Track dates, shift types, start/end times, breaks, and hours across a full month, with monthly totals.

 🐦

Sleep Tracker

Log bedtime, wake time, hours slept, sleep quality, and naps — built for rotating and night shifts.

 🛝

Shift Swap Sheet

Request, approve, and record shift swaps with coworker and manager sign-off lines.

 💰

Pay Calculator

Work out gross pay, overtime, differentials, tax, and weekly/monthly estimates per shift.

How to use

  1. Open any form above in your browser.
  2. Fill it in on screen, or print it blank and fill it in by hand.
  3. To save as PDF: choose File > Print, then Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF). Background colors are removed automatically for cheap printing.
  4. Print at 100% on US Letter or A4 paper. Each page is sized to fit one sheet.

Low-energy design: soft colors, high contrast, and plenty of whitespace so the forms are easy to read when you're tired after a shift. Made for nurses, factory workers, first responders, and anyone on a rotating schedule.
